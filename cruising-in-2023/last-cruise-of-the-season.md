Saturday 7th October

Trip to Bangor for a visit to the Lifeboat Station

0930 Leave Carrickfergus Marina

1130 Visit to Bangor Lifeboat Station

1400 Lunch in Jenny Watts’ Pub

Back in time to watch Ireland v Scotland on the big screen in the Clubhouse

Notes:<br>1 You are responsible for your own berthing fees in Bangor Marina<br>2 You pay your own bill at Jenny Watts
