Sun, Sea and Sizzle.

Calling all skippers, crews and friends.

Fancy a sail, food and a free overnight stay?

Then don't miss out on our<br>Glenarm BBQ

21-22 July 2023

BBQ will be held on Friday evening 21st July.

Yachts will leave Carrickfergus at midday on Friday, to pick up the favourable tide at Blackhead. We should arrive at Glenarm in time for the BBQ at approx. 1930.

We can return 0900 on Saturday, to catch the flood tide to Carrickfergus.

Bring your own food, we provide the bbq grill.

We need to know numbers so please sign up at the bar.
