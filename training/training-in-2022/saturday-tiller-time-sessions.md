Saturday 10th September to 8th October 2022<br>14:00 to 16:00

Following on from our RYA Youth summer courses the club invites all those who attended to join us for some Saturday afternoon tiller time to develop those basic sailing skills.

This is an opportunity for the club's young junior sailors up to the age of 16 who recently completed the youth courses to gain more sailing experience under supervision and have some fun on the water.The invitation is also open to all young sailors in the club who perhaps completed previous courses and who are current members to participate.

We would also request the parents to attend during the sessions to assist and gain some boat knowledge themselves to assist their children at future sessions and events.

The sessions will be aimed at recreational sailing to improve skills and will not be racing focused.

Five sessions will be held on the water from 2:00 pm until 4:00 pm on the above dates.

The first session will start at 1:00 pm on Saturday 10th September<br>to allow a parent and junior briefing.

There will be no charge for these sessions but participants must register in advance by email to [training@carrickfergussc.org](mailto:training@carrickfergussc.org)
