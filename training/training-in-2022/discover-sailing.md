## For prospective members of the public

Our Discover Sailing day for those interested in experiencing a taster sailing session and possibly joining the club will be held on Saturday 7th May and due to the marina closure and lack of keel boats in the water will be aimed at dinghy sailing for both juniors (aged 8 & over) and adults.

## For Members

If you know any friends or family who may be interested please let them know the date!<br>All participants will be required to pre register online in advance for one of the specific sessions.

We will require a large number of volunteers for helms, safety crew, meeters and greeters and also to help with hospitality and membership enquires on the day. If you can assist please contact the Commodore Trevor McCaig ASAP.
