## Dinghy Level 1 - Start Sailing

An introduction to sailing dinghies for complete beginners, covering everything you need to know to get afloat under supervision

Required experience - None

Minimum duration - Approximately 2 days, 16 hours or equivalent number of session

Minimum age - None (although under 16s may prefer to learn through the Youth Sailing Scheme)

Content - How to sail in all directions, including an awareness of launching and recovering

Certificate issuing criteria - Able to sail in light winds under supervision

Format - Afloat

## Dinghy Level 2 - Basic Skills

Build your confidence and develop your skills. By the end of this course you'll be a competent sailor in light winds

Required experience - Sailing skills to the standard of Level 1 Start Sailing

Minimum duration - Approximately 2 days, 16 hours or equivalent number of sessions

Minimum age - None (although under 16s may prefer to learn through the Youth Sailing Scheme)

Content - Rigging, launching and building confidence sailing in all directions. Capsize recovery and essential safety knowledge.

Certificate issuing criteria - Able to sail confidently on all points of sail and make decisions in good conditions.

Format - Afloat
