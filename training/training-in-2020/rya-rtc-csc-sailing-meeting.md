Tuesday 25<sup>th</sup> February 2020 @ 1900 Hrs (upstairs in the club house) 

As principal of the Recognised Training Centre at Carrickfergus Sailing Club, I would like to invite parents of our junior members, junior sailors, adult members who wish to assist in this season’s activities, and adults interested in learning to sail dinghies and/or keel boats to discuss the proposed plans for this coming season. 

We have many new developments and additions at the clubover the winter which will enhance sailing for all.

The agenda will include:

Junior training/ RYA courses and club summer training schemes.

Dinghy sailing and racing. 

Bright Friday Night sailing.

Wednesday dinghy racing.

RYA Women on the water program.

RYA / CSC Discover Sailing (formerly “Push the Boat Out”.)

I and the Hon. Sailing Secretary look forward to seeing you all there. 

Brian Wilson

Principal RTC at CSC
