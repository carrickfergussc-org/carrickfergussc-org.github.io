Weekend Water Sports - ***Booking essential***.

Friday nights 6.30pm - 8.30pm. Starting 11th June 2021

We would like to see all our members, no matter what age enjoy ‘race free’ evenings on the water. Whether you want to sail, kayak, paddle board or windsurf come down and join in. There will be patrol boats on the water for emergencies only and our dinghy’s will be available for use.

Bring along your own kayak or SUP and if you have any equipment you would be willing to lend out it would be greatly appreciated.

Changing rooms will be open but numbers will be limited due to Covid Restriction. If you require private changing, please let us know.

***All participants must be members and weekly booking is essential for each member attending.***

All under 18’s should be accompanied by a parent/ guardian member and should be able to launch and recover themselves.

Under 18’s with less experience must be accompanied on the water by a competent parent/guardian member.
