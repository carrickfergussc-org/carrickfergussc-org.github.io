First Aid courses, primarily for those age 16+ years who wish to volunteer to crew on Safety Boats or assist with sailing events, but in fact for any member who wants to be prepared to deal with injuries or incidents on the water or at home.

Three courses have been organised this year so far:

<ul><li>Sunday 16th  February</li><li>Sunday 1st March</li><li>Sunday 7th June</li></ul>

The courses generally start at 09:15 hrs in the Club and run until 16:00.

The courses will again be taken by Colin Craig and successful participants will received an RYA Certificate and pack on completion.

Each course is limited to a maximum of 8 participants and aimed at members who may already have obtained their Power Boat Level 2 qualification and other current members who may simply wish to assist with safety or generally at club events.

The course fee for CSC Members is<br>£ 35 and includes the RYA pack.
