We will be running an Easter training course for our junior members with **RYA Level 2 **and above.<br><br>The course will run from **8th-10th April** from **1000-1600** daily with cost being £60.

The course will be split into groups, those wanting to refresh sailing skills and those who want to focus on racing techniques. The course will be limited to 16 participants.  Online entry will be available shortly.
