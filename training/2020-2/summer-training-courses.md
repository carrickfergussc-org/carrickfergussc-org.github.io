Excellent news -   We can now announce 4 weeks of Junior sailing sessions at CSC for Summer 2020.  It will be available to book from next week, however payment for courses is to be made in cash on the first day of the weekly session.

Each week will provide excellent sailing sessions for pods of 10 Juniors with fully qualified instructors to ensure all attendees will gain experience on the water while adhering to government restrictions for Covid -19.

Our CSC instructors Andrew Penney, Jackson Smyth , Katie Nelson and Andrew Byres will be joined by our new instructor Ellen McCarlie.

We have taken the decision to limit the sessions to members only and those who have completed a minimum of RYA level 2.

## All courses

Limited to pods of 10 children in each session.

Social distancing will be maintained and children will be required to sanitised hands regularly.

Parents written permission to override social distancing requirements to allow the rescue of a child. and will be limited contact of no more than 10 mins 

Children needing to borrow a club boats will be required to hire for the week it will be assigned and not shared.  

Trainer will be required to clean down training room and safety boats at the end of session.

Children with symptoms will not be allowed to attend session.  

Instructors will be provided with PPE.
