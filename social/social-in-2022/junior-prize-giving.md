## **<strong>Saturday 26th November 2022**</strong>

## 4:00 till 6:30 pm

Our presentation of prizes to juniors who competed in the following events will take place<br>on Saturday 26th November. There will be some entertainment for the juniors, accompanied by pizza and soft drinks for those wishing to stay following the presentation.

Our bar will be open for the parents and adult pizzas can be ordered at the bar<br>for anyone wishing to make an evening of it.

**Early Sunday PY Handicap Dinghy Series**<br>May to July

**Late Wednesday PY2 Dinghy Fleet**<br>July to August

**Autumn Sunday PY Dinghy Fleet**<br>September to October

We would also love to see all juniors who took part in our **RYA summer courses** and Autumn ***Tiller Time ***sessions come along and meet up again.

To assist us please email **[sailing@carrickfergussc.org](mailto:sailing@carrickfergussc.org)****by no later than Wednesday 23rd**<br>to confirm if you will be attending and how many adults and juniors in your group/family
