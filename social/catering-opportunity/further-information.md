# The Caterer shall be responsible for:

<ol class="lettered-list"><li>provide a general high quality catering service in the clubhouse to include the services of hot and cold meals, snacks, refreshments and non-alcoholic hot beverages to members and guests. All alcohol consumed in the course of the catering provision will be purchased through the Club bar;</li><li>ensure that the food service areas are open and sufficiently staffed during such times as shall be agreed from time to time with CSC;</li><li>be responsible for the ordering, and supply of items of food and beverages of good and sound quality in accordance with lists, prominently displayed in the clubhouse, and at competitive prices having regard to the quality of service required by CSC;</li><li>not without the consent of CSC to store any food or materials in any areas which are not intended for use in connection with the catering operation thereat;</li><li>at all times maintain high standards of hygiene and general cleanliness in the preparation, handling and serving of food and beverages and in the use and maintenance of fittings, utensils and equipment and ensure compliance with all statutory requirements [including the Food Safety (NI) Order 1991 and the Food Safety (General Food Hygiene) Regulations (NI) 2006 and all relevant Codes of Practice and Guides];</li><li>keep clean at all times those areas designated for the sole use, during the catering operation, including the kitchen, equipment, utensils, carpet, tables, chairs and floors.<br>Remove and clean extraction filter panels on a quarterly basis.</li><li>Provide a sufficient number of competent, customer friendly and properly trained staff.<br>This to include adequate cover for holidays, illness or other such events.<br>Ensure that all staff shall be suitably dressed and shall be clean, sober and civil;</li><li>in the event of disorderly conduct occurring, to take immediately such effective steps as are possible.<br>Report immediately any incidents to an officer of the club or to the bar person.</li><li>Pay rates of wages and observe hours and regulations of labour no less favourable than those established for the catering industry and prevailing in the local area; ## Exceptions to the Caterer’s provision of the Services

<ol class="lettered-list"><li>The Caterer agrees that, in the event of the Club wishing to raise money for a specific purpose, the Club shall have the right to ask Club Members to supply meals and refreshments for such events, which will be limited to 6 occasions per year.</li><li>The Caterer acknowledges that, on Club Regatta Day, the Club’s members have the right to provide food and refreshments for competing crews after racing.</li><li>The Caterer agrees that, in addition to alcoholic beverages, tea and coffee may be purchased by members from the Club bar.</li><li>Club BBQs.</li></ol>

## CSC shall, during the term of the franchise, observe the following conditions:-

<ol class="lettered-list"><li>it shall not permit the supply of a catering service of any description by any other person by way of trade in the club house;</li><li>it shall maintain in good repair and condition the exterior and structural parts of the clubhouse and mechanical, electrical and other services;</li><li>it shall be responsible for the supply of all furniture in the clubhouse;</li><li>it shall minimise as far as reasonably practicable any disruption consequent upon any repairs or works at or to the club house;</li></ol>
