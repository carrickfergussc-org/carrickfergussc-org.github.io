UPDATE: The start for this series has to be put back a week to 7th June due to the marina dredging work.

Keelboat racing is scheduled to start on 7th June and running right through the summer (excluding 12th July week) as a separate Tuesday and Thursday series until the end of August.

Classes available for NHC Unrestricted, NHC Restricted and Ruffian 23.

For boats in NHC Unrestricted with a current valid IRC certificate there will also be dual scoring under IRC.
