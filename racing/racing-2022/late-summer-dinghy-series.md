The Late Summer Series for Dinghies will run on Wednesday evenings from 20th July to 31st August.

It is open to all dinghies for which a RYA PY number is available.

There will be two classes roughly divided as follows:<br>PY 1 - RS40, Lasers and similar<br>PY 2 - Toppers, Qubas and similar

Other classes of boats will be allocated to a class as appropriate.
