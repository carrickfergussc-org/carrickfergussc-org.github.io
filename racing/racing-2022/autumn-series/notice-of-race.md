<ol><li>Rules<ol><li>The event is governed by the rules as defined in The Racing Rules of Sailing.</li></ol></li><li>Sailing Instructions<ol><li>The sailing instructions will be available at [www.carrickfergussc.org](http://www.carrickfergussc.org).</li></ol></li><li>Communication<ol><li>The online official notice board is located at [www.carrickfergussc.org](http://www.carrickfergussc.org).</li><li>All keelboats shall carry a VHF radio capable of communicating on 37.</li><li>On the water, the race committee will make courtesy broadcasts to competitors on VHF radio. The channel will be stated in the SIs.</li><li>While racing, except in an emergency, a boat shall not make voice or data transmissions and shall not receive voice or data communication that is not available to all boats.</li></ol></li><li>Eligibility and Entry<ol><li>The event is open to all boats of the Keelboat and PY Dinghy classes.</li><li>The RYA NHC system applies to boats racing under NHC Unrestricted and NHC Unrestricted.</li><li>Classes are defined as follows: NHC Unrestricted, NHC Restricted, Ruffian 23, RS400, PY Dinghy</li><li>Boats may enter the event by registering online at [www.carrickfergussc.org](http://www.carrickfergussc.org).</li><li>To be considered an entry in the event, a boat shall complete all registration requirements and pay all fees.</li><li>Late entries will be accepted at the discretion of the race committee.</li></ol></li><li>Fees<ol><li>Entry fees are as follows:<br>Non-members - £20<br>Member/Associate - £10<br>Junior - £5</li></ol></li><li>Schedule<ol><li>Dates of racing: Sundays from 11th September to 30th October</li><li>Number of races: Keelboats - 8, Dinghies - 16</li><li>The scheduled time of the warning signal for the first race each day is 1410 for Keelboat classes and 1425 for Dinghy classes</li><li>No warning signal will be made after 1600.</li></ol></li><li>Venue <ol><li>Racing will take place in Belfast Lough near Carrickfergus Sailing Club.</li></ol></li><li>Courses<ol><li>Courses for Keelboats will be around fixed marks of Carrickfergus with a Shore Based Start/Finish</li><li>Courses for RS400 and PY Dinghies will be Windward/Leeward style courses with a Committee Boat Start/Finish.</li></ol></li><li>Penalty System<ol><li>For the RS400 and PY Dinghy classes, RRS 44.1 is changed so that the Two-Turns Penalty is replaced by the One-Turn Penalty. </li><li>The Post-Race Penalty, Advisory Hearing and RYA Arbitration of the RYA Rules Disputes procedures shall apply (for information on the procedures, go to www.rya.org.uk/go/rules). The outcome of an RYA Arbitration can be referred to a protest committee, but an arbitration cannot be reopened or appealed.</li></ol></li><li>Scoring<ol><li>Two races are required to be completed to constitute a series.</li><li>(a) When fewer than 5 races have been completed, a boat’s series score is the total of her race scores.<br>(b) When from 5 to 8 races have been completed, a boat’s series score is the total of her race scores excluding her worst score.<br>(c) When from 9 to 12 races have been completed, a boat’s series score is the total of her race scores excluding her worst score.<br>(d) When 13 or more races have been completed, a boat’s series score is the total of her race scores excluding her two worst scores.</li><li>Rule A5.2 is changed so that, a boat that did not come to the starting area, came to the starting area but did not start, did not sail the course, did not finish, retired or was disqualified shall be scored points for the finishing place one more than the number of boats that came to the starting area.</li></ol></li><li>Risk Statement<ol><li>Rule 3 of the Racing Rules of Sailing states: ‘The responsibility for a boat's decision to participate in a race or to continue racing is hers alone.’</li><li>Sailing is by its nature an unpredictable sport and therefore inherently involves an element of risk.
