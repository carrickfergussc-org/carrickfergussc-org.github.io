Racing for Keelboats (YTC and Ruffian 23) and Dinghies (RS400 and PY).

Sundays from 17th March to 28th April 2024, excluding 31st March.

Shore-based starts around fixed marks for Keelboats.

Committee Boat start and Windward/Leeward for RS400 and PY Dnghies.
