Racing for Keelboats (YTC and Ruffian 23) and Dinghies (RS400 and PY).

<ul>
<li>Early Tuesday - Keelboat - 7th May to 2nd July.</li>

<li>Early Wednesday - Dinghy - 8th May to 3rd July.</li>

<li>Early Thursday - Keelboat - 9th May to 4th July.</li>

<li>Late Tuesday - Keelboat - 16th July to 27th August.</li>

<li>Late Wednesday - Dinghy - 17th July to 28th August.</li>

<li>Late Thursday - Keelboat - 18th July to 29th August.</li>
</ul>

Shore-based starts around fixed marks for Keelboats.

Committee Boat start and Windward/Leeward for RS400 and PY Dnghies.
