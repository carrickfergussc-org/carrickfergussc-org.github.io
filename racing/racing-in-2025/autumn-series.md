Racing for Ruffian 23, YTC Restricted & Unrestricted, RS400 and PY Dinghy.

Racing every Sunday from 7th September through until 26th October.

Up to 2 races each day.

Fixed marks for Keelboats and Windward/Leeward (where feasible) for Dinghies.
