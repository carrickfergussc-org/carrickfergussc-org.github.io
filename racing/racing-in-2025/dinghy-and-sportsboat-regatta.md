One days racing for Sportsboats, RS200/400, PY Dinghy and Hansa 303 on Saturday 27th September.

Four races scheduled.

Windward/Leeward courses.

Briefing at 1000, First start at 1100.

Food and entertainment in the clubhouse afterwards.

Entry fee per boat:<br>£15 - Double Hander<br>£10 - Single Hander

Meal Ticket per person - £15.
