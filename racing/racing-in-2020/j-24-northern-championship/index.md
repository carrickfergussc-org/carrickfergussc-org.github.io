This event has been provisionally rescheduled to the new date of 8/9 August .

The club is pleased to be hosting the Northern Championship for the International J/24 Class Association of Ireland on Saturday 8th and Sunday 9th August 2020 .

Launching will be available on Friday during the day and Saturday morning and is included in the entry fee. Boats will be berthed in the Marina/Harbour and be prepared to raft alongside other J/24s.

There will be a championship dinner on the Saturday evening. Tickets for this and other catering will be available to purchase nearer the time or at registration.

We have space on the club grounds for pitching tents on grassy areas and for parking caravans/campers in the carpark.

Entry can be completed using the links below. Please fill in the Entry Form to give us the details of your entry, and then proceed to the Entry Payment link to make the payment for your entry using Paypal or a Credit/Debit card. Bank details can be made available to do a bank transfer if required.

For further information email [Steve Atkinson](mailto:steve.atkinson@windswept.org.uk) or [Richard McCaig](mailto:richard.mccaig@osborneking.com)
