Early and Late Series for all classes.

RS400 and PY Dinghy on Wednesdays with two races each night.

<ul><li>Early Series - 3rd May to 5th July.</li><li>Late Series - 19th July to 30th August.</li></ul>

YTC Keelboays and Ruffian 23 on Tuesdays and Thursdays.

<ul><li>Early Tuesday - 2nd May to 4th July.</li><li>Early Thursday - 4th May to 6th July.</li><li>Late Tuesday - 18th July to 29th August.</li><li>Late Thursday - 20th July to 31st August.</li></ul>
