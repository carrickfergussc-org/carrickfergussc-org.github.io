Racing over two days for Full and 4.2 sail Topper classes at Carrickfergus Sailing Club

Saturday 29th - Sunday 30th April 2023

There is also an event WhatsApp group which you can access by following the link below: [https://chat.whatsapp.com/BvmDrDNIwmg0UJGvcPEO9w](https://chat.whatsapp.com/BvmDrDNIwmg0UJGvcPEO9w)

We encourage you to sign up to this channel which will be used to informally communicate things happening around the Club during the event.  To keep things simple, it is one way communication only from the event team.  If you have something urgent to share (e.g. someone has forgotten a buoyancy aid, or a missing packet of emergency Fruit Pastilles) please reach out to the event team and they can post on your behalf.
