Finally we are able to publish what racing we can start to do this summer!

There will be racing on different days for different classes as follows

Early Saturdays for Junior sailors<br>Start on the 1st of May for 5 weeks endign on 29th May<br>Up to three races with the first start at 1415

Junior Saturdays will continue on as a new longer series from 12th Jun to the 31st July

Sundays for RS400 and other dinghies<br>Starting on the 2nd May for 5 weeks<br>Two races each Sunday with the first start at 1400

Wednesdays for RS400 and other dinghies<br>Starting on the 9th of June through to the 25th of August<br>Two races each week with the first start at 1915

Tuesday and Thursday evenings for Keelboats<br>One race each evening starting at 1915 <br>Starting with the first race on Thursday 6th May and then every Tuesday and Thursday until the 1st July.

Tuesday and Thursday evenings will continue for Keelboats into the  late series from 6th July through to 2nd September
