**Venue:** Carrickfergus SC (Belfast Lough)

**Dates:** 29–30 August 2026

**Fleets:** Topper 5.3 & 4.2

**Format:** 6 trapezoid races over 2 days (1 discard if 4+ completed)

**Entry:** £65 | Capped at 50 boats | Closes 26 August 2026

**Prizes:** Top 3 in 5.3 Youth/Junior (Male & Female) and 4.2 overall
