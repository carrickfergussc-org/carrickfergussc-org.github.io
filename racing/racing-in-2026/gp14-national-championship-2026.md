Dates: Saturday 1st & Sunday 2nd August 2026 (Registration also open Friday evening, 31st July)

Location: Carrickfergus Sailing Club, racing on Belfast Lough

Format: 6 scheduled races over 2 days (Windward/Leeward and Triangle courses)

Format & Courses: A 6-race series featuring Windward/Leeward (W/L) with Triangle, Triangles with W/L, or standard Windward/Leeward courses

Fees: £55.00 per boat standard entry; £27.50 concession for youth helms (under 22) sailing with a youth crew

Prizes: Perpetual trophies and keepsake prizes across Gold, Silver, and Bronze fleets, plus a dedicated 1st Place Youth prize
