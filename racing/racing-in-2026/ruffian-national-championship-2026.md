**Welcome to the 2026 Ruffian Irish National Championship!**

⛵️[Results](https://www.sailwave.com/results/carrickfergussc/2026RuffianChampionship.html) 

We are absolutely thrilled to welcome the Ruffian fleet to Carrickfergus Sailing Club from **Friday, 26th June to Sunday, 28th June 2026** for three days of brilliant racing, great laughs, and classic CSC hospitality.

Whether you’re a regular on our waters or traveling to join us, we want to make your weekend as easy and fun as possible. Here is everything you need to know:

### ⛵ On the Water

We have a fantastic 6-race series planned out on the Lough. The action kicks off on Friday morning, with the first warning signal at **10:55 AM**.

### Hassle-Free Logistics

We’ve got you covered from the moment you arrive:

<ul>
<li>**Free Boat Lift:** Free lift-in on Thursday and lift-out on Sunday for all travelling boats.</li>

<li>**Free Parking:** On-site trailer storage is completely free.</li>

<li>**Berthing:** Visiting boats can enjoy 3 nights of berthing right here for just **£30**.</li>
</ul>

### Food, Drinks & Good Times

The clubhouse will be the hub of all the weekend's action, so you won't have to wander far to fuel up:

<ul>
<li>**Breakfast:** Hearty, reasonably priced breakfasts served Friday and Saturday morning (8:00 AM – 10:00 AM) to get you ready for the line.</li>

<li>**Friday Night:** Relax and unwind with catering right at the club after day one.</li>

<li>**The Big Saturday Night Roast:** Join everyone for a delicious 2-course roast dinner at the club for just **£20**. *(Just a heads-up: this needs to be pre-booked and paid for online so our chef can get a headcount!)*</li>

<li>**Sunday Apres-Sail:** We’ll have plenty of food and drinks flowing to celebrate the winners and wrap up the weekend.</li>
</ul>

### ✍️ How to Join Us

Ready to race? Grab the early bird discount and save yourself some spending money for the bar!

<ul>
<li>**Early Bird Entry:** £80</li>

<li>**Late Entry (after 19th June):** £120</li>
</ul>

You can download the official **Notice of Race** below to check out the finer details, and when you're ready, head over to our **Online Entry** form to lock in your spot.

We can't wait to see you all on the start line for a fantastic weekend of sailing!
