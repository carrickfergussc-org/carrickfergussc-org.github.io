There will be informal racing run for less experienced Junior Sailors starting from Sunday 6th September.

There will potentially be multiple races each day depending on numbers, time and weather conditions.

Instructions for Starting and Courses will be notified to competitors before they go afloat.

Launching and Racing will take place after the starts for the main Autumn Series racing for Keelboats and Dinghies has cleared the area in front of the club.

Boats will be available to borrow and must be filled in as part of the Registration process.

Entry for this will be free for Junior Members.
