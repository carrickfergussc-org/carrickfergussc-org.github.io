**RYANI Youth Championships 2021 proudly hosted by Carrickfergus Sailing Club**

## About this event

The RYANI Youth Championships is open to sailors under the age of 19 in: ILCA7 / ILCA 6 / ILCA 4 / 420 / 29er / Topper (both rigs) / RS Feva / Mirror / Optimist

By making a choice to come to the club and take part in the event, understand that you increase your risk and therefore household risk of exposure to COVID 19.

Applications will not be accepted after** 1200 (noon) on Monday 6th Sept**.

## Daily Format

The day to day operational plan may be different from before as we adapt to the Covid-19 guidelines and mitigations.

There will be no on-site registration for this event. Competitors will be required to collect a launching trolley tag from the Race Office on arrival.

There will be no briefing. This will be recorded and shared acorss event media channels.

### Race Schedule:

Saturday 11th September Races 1, 2 and 3

Sunday 12th September Races 4, 5 and 6

The scheduled time of the first warning signal of the first race on Saturday is 1155.

The scheduled time of the first warning signal of the first race on Sunday is 1055.

An additional race may be run on Saturday at the discretion of the Race Committee.

On the last scheduled day of racing, no warning signal will be made after 1500.
