<ol><li>Rules<ol><li>The event is governed by the rules as defined in The Racing Rules of Sailing.</li></ol></li><li>Sailing Instructions<ol><li>The sailing instructions will be available at [www.carrickfergussc.org](http://www.carrickfergussc.org).</li></ol></li><li>Communication<ol><li>The online official notice board is located at [www.carrickfergussc.org](http://www.carrickfergussc.org).</li><li>All boats shall carry a VHF radio capable of communicating on 37.</li><li>On the water, the race committee will make courtesy broadcasts to competitors on VHF radio. The channel will be stated in the SIs.</li><li>While racing, except in an emergency, a boat shall not make voice or data transmissions and shall not receive voice or data communication that is not available to all boats.</li></ol></li><li>Eligibility and Entry<ol><li>The event is open to all boats of the Keelboat and Ruffian 23 class.</li><li>The National Handicap for Cruisers (NHC) applies to boats racing under Unrestricted and Restricted Sail.</li><li>Classes are defined as follows:<br>Unrestricted - NHC Keelboats with spinnakers<br>Restricted - NHC Keelboats without spinnakers<br>Ruffian 23 - One Design Ruffian 23 Keelboats</li><li>All Competitors must be Sailing Members/Associates of Carrickfergus Sailing Club</li><li>Boats may enter the event by registering online at [www.carrickfergussc.org](http://www.carrickfergussc.org).</li><li>To be considered an entry in the event, a boat shall complete all registration requirements.</li><li>Late entries will be accepted at the discretion of the Race Committee.</li></ol></li><li>Fees<ol><li>No entry fees.</li></ol></li><li>Schedule<ol><li>Dates of racing:<br>Tuesday Series - 11th May to 29th June<br>Thursday Series - 6th May - 1st July</li><li>Number of races:<br>Tuesday Series - 7<br>Thursday Series - 8</li><li>The scheduled time of the warning signal for the first race each day is 1910.</li><li>On the last scheduled day of racing no warning signal will be made after 2000.</li></ol></li><li>Venue <ol><li>Racing will take place in Belfast Lough near Carrickfergus Sailing Club.</li></ol></li><li>Courses<ol><li>Courses will be round fixed marks laid near Carrickfergus Sailing Club</li></ol></li><li>Penalty System<ol><li>The Post-Race Penalty, Advisory Hearing and RYA Arbitration of the RYA Rules Disputes procedures shall apply (for information on the procedures, go to www.rya.org.uk/go/rules).
