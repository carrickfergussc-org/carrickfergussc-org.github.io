<ol><li>Rules<ol><li>The event is governed by the rules as defined in The Racing Rules of Sailing.</li><li>The current Portsmouth Yardstick Scheme applies to boats racing under PY.</li></ol></li><li>Sailing Instructions<ol><li>The sailing instructions will be available at [www.carrickfergussc.org](http://www.carrickfergussc.org).</li></ol></li><li>Communication<ol><li>The online official notice board is located at [www.carrickfergussc.org](http://www.carrickfergussc.org).</li></ol></li><li>Eligibility and Entry<ol><li>The event is open to all boats of the RS400 and other Dinghy classes.</li><li>If there are sufficient entries of particular classes of boat then they may be divided out as separate classes.</li><li>Boats may enter the event by registering online at [www.carrickfergussc.org](http://www.carrickfergussc.org).</li><li>To be considered an entry in the event, a boat shall complete all registration requirements and pay all fees.</li><li>Late entries will be accepted at the discretion of the race committee.</li></ol></li><li>Fees<ol><li>Entry fees are as follows:<br>CSC Member, Sailing Associate, 18-25 Associate, Junior Associate - £0<br>Non-Members - £25</li></ol></li><li>Schedule<ol><li>Dates of racing: Sunday 2nd May to 30th May 2021. </li><li>Number of races: 10</li><li>The scheduled time of the warning signal for the first race each day is 13:57.</li><li>On the last scheduled day of racing no warning signal will be made after 16:00.</li></ol></li><li>Venue <ol><li>Racing will take place in Belfast Lough near Carrickfergus Sailing Club.</li></ol></li><li>Courses<ol><li>The course will be notified to competitors before they go afloat.</li></ol></li><li>Penalty System<ol><li>The Post-Race Penalty, Advisory Hearing and RYA Arbitration of the RYA Rules Disputes procedures shall apply (for information on the procedures, go to www.rya.org.uk/go/rules).
