Saturday 14th to Sunday 15th August 2021

Supported by: Osborne King and Mid & East Antrim Borough Council

Carrickfergus Sailing Club, County Antrim

Membership of a Class Association is a requirement for this event. Further information on [the RS Ireland website](https://www.rsireland.com/membership/).

Competitors should make themselves aware of the [guidance and restrictions on the Covid-19 pages of the website](https://www.carrickfergussc.org/covid-19/)
