The Boat Park is available to be used by members to store their boats and trailers through out the summer and winter.

Boats and trailer must be clearly identified with the Boat and Owner's name

Because of the expected increase in usage for the winter of 2021/22 due to the Marina dredging an application for storage must be completed before boats will be permitted to be stored.
