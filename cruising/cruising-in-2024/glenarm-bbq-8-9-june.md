Carrickfergus Sailing Club

Glenarm BBQ

8th - 9th June

The Glenarm BBQ will be held on Saturday evening.

Yachts will leave Carrickfergus at midday on Saturday to pick up the favourable tide at Blackhead.

We should arrive at Glenarm in time for the BBQ at approx. 1930

Return 0830 on Sunday, to catch the flood tide to Carrickfergus.

Bring your own food, we provide the BBQ.

Distance: 25nm.

We need to know numbers, so please sign up on the sheet at the CSC Bar.
