Pack a picnic and join us for the Copeland Island Cruise & Picnic

Sunday 6th August

Weather permitting we will sail to Copeland Island and anchor in Chapel Bay for lunch.

Distance - approximately 10 nautical miles. 

We leave Carrickfergus Marina at 10.30

High water Belfast 1531 DST

NB: Care should be taken to avoid the Rid Rock when rounding Carn Point.

Each boat should provide their own picnic food and drinks.

 If you have a dinghy, please bring it for getting ashore.

<ul><li>As this is a club event lifejackets/buoyancy aids should be worn when afloat.</li><li>All vessels must have their own insurance.</li><li>First aid kits should be carried.</li><li>While information is given in good faith safe pilotage of the vessel is the Skipper’s sole responsibility.</li></ul>

Trip leader: Wendy Grant
