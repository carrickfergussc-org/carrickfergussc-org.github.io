## Notes for applications

<ul>
<li>For anyone taking up new membership you should be aware that membership is for the year from 1st January to 31st December, NOT for a year from when you apply and get elected.</li>

<li>Application forms must include names and signatures for the Proposer and Seconder who must be people that the applicant is know to.</li>

<li>Applications for Sailing Associate must include a letter from your Home Club confirming that you are a fully paid up member currently.</li>

<li>Applications for Senior or Senior Family categories must have held Ordinary or Ordinary Family membership for a full year previously.</li>
</ul>

## Definition of membership categories and their privileges

This information is a summary of the detail contained in the [Articles of Association](https://www.carrickfergussc.org/rules-and-regulations/articles-of-association-and-bye-laws/):

**Member** - A person admitted to membership of the Company in accordance with these Presents

<ul>
<li>**Ordinary Member** - A person over the age of eighteen upon admission to membership - full use of all the Club facilities;</li>

<li>**Family Member** - Either or both of an adult couple living together, or any person of a family unit containing a child under eighteen years of age - full use of all the Club facilities subject to any restriction imposed by law as to the presence of minors on licensed premises;</li>

<li>**Senior Member** - An Ordinary Member of one year’s standing over the age of sixty five years on the Subscription Date - full use of all the Club facilities;</li>

<li>**Senior Family Member** - Either one of a couple where one of the couple is a family member of one year’s standing over the age of sixty five years on the day the Subscription Date - full use of all the Club facilities;</li>
</ul>

**Associate** - A person, not being a Member, permitted the use of the Club premises and facilities in accordance with these Presents upon the payment of a fee or otherwise;

<ul>
<li>**Sailing Associate** - A voting member of another Yacht Club affiliated to a recognised Sailing Governing Body permitted use of Club facilities at the discretion of the Management Committee - full use of all the Club facilities except storage of boats or equipment unless expressly permitted by the Management Committee;</li>

<li>**House Associate** - A person, aged over eighteen years of age, permitted to use the Clubhouse facilities - use of the Clubhouse facilities only;</li>

<li>**Associate 18 - 25** - A person aged eighteen years and over, but not having attained the age of twenty five years - full use of all the Club facilities;</li>

<li>**Junior Associate** - A person aged eight years and over, but not having attained the age of eighteen years - full use of all the Club facilities subject to any restriction imposed by law as to the presence of minors on licensed premises</li>
</ul>
