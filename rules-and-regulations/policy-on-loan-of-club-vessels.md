<ol><li>The club owns a number of sailing vessels including:<br>a. 9 Toppers<br>b. 8 Qubas<br>c. 3 Bahias</li><li>Suitably qualified/experienced Club Members may book the use of these craft for racing in one of the Club’s race series. For this purpose “member” means:<br>a. Ordinary member<br>b. Family member<br>c. 18-25 associate<br>d. Junior associate</li><li>The applicant must be suitably qualified (normally RYA stage 3)</li><li>In loaning a boat the member must undertake:<br>a. To keep the boat in good order<br>b. To ensure the boat is available for Club courses, informal training sessions and other Club events where the boat if required.</li><li>For a series (normally 8 – 10 sessions) a charge of £50 is payable. It must be noted that, in entering a boat for a race series, the Club rules regarding membership status of crew must be observed.</li><li>Application for loan must be made to the Hon. Sailing Secretary, or RTC Principal, who will ascertain the suitability of the member for this loan. A form (attached below) must be completed for loan for a series.</li><li>The Club also owns a number of other vessels: 3 RIBs and a workboat (“Sublime”). These boats are not normally available for loan.</li></ol>
