It is the policy of **Carrickfergus Sailing Club **that all participants, coaches, instructors, officials, parents and  volunteers show respect and understanding for each other, treat everyone equally within the context of  the sport and conduct themselves in a way that reflects the principles of the club. The aim is for all  participants to enjoy their sport and to improve performance. 

**Abusive language, swearing, intimidation, aggressive behaviour or lack of respect for others and their property will not be tolerated and may lead to disciplinary action. **

**<span class="has-inline-color has-vivid-red-color"><span style="text-decoration: underline;">Participants</span> - young sailors and powerboaters </span>**

<ul><li>Listen to and accept what you are asked to do to improve your performance and keep you safe</li><li>Respect other participants, coaches, instructors, officials and volunteers</li><li>Abide by the rules and play fairly </li><li>Do your best at all times </li><li>Never bully others either in person, by phone, by text or online </li><li>Take care of all property belonging to other participants, the club/class or its members </li></ul>

**<span class="has-inline-color has-vivid-red-color"><span style="text-decoration: underline;">Parents</span></span> **

<ul><li>Support your child’s involvement and help them enjoy their sport </li><li>Help your child to recognise good performance, not just results </li><li>Never force your child to take part in sport </li><li>Never punish or belittle a child for losing or making mistakes </li><li>Encourage and guide your child to accept responsibility for their own conduct and performance</li><li>Respect and support the coach </li><li>Accept officials’ judgements and recognise good performance by all participants </li><li>Use established procedures where there is a genuine concern or dispute </li><li>Inform the club or event organisers of relevant medical information </li><li>Ensure that your child wears suitable clothing and has appropriate food and drink</li><li>Provide contact details and be available when required </li><li>Take responsibility for your child’s safety and conduct in and around the clubhouse/event venue </li></ul>

**<span style="text-decoration: underline;"><span class="has-inline-color has-vivid-red-color">Coaches, Instructors, Officials and Volunteers </span></span>**

<ul><li>Consider the welfare and safety of participants before the development of performance</li><li>Encourage participants to value their performance and not just results </li><li>Promote fair play and never condone cheating </li><li>Ensure that all activities are appropriate to the age, ability and experience of those taking part</li><li>Build relationships based on mutual trust and respect </li><li>Work in an open environment </li><li>Avoid unnecessary physical contact with young people</li><li>Be an excellent role model and display consistently high standards of behaviour and appearance</li><li>Do not drink alcohol or smoke when working directly with young people </li><li>Communicate clearly with parents and participants </li><li>Be aware of any relevant medical information </li><li>Follow RYA and club/class guidelines and policies </li><li>Holders of RYA Instructor and Coach qualifications must also comply with the RYA Code of Ethics and  Conduct </li><li>Holders of RYA Race Official appointments must also comply with the RYA Race Officials Code of  Conduct. </li></ul>

If you are concerned that someone is not following the Code of Conduct, you should inform your Club Welfare Officer or the person in charge of the activity.
