## **Tuesday 27<sup>th</sup> December**

Join us for the annual post Christmas cruise to Belfast.

Boats to leave Carrickfergus Marina or harbour and assemble by 11.30 am at Buoy No13 to form a convoy and proceed to Belfast Harbour. In the event of adverse weather members can make their way by land to McHugh's for 12:30/1:00 pm.
