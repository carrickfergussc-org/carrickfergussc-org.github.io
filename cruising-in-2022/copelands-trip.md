***Unfortunately due to the weather forecast the Copelands Trip has been CANCELLED***

We plan to do a picnic trip to the Copeland Islands on Sat 3<sup>rd</sup> September. We plan to leave Carrickfergus at 0930 and to set off for return at 1500

## TIDES

HW Belfast: 1632 (BST) (half spring)

Note strong tides in Donaghadee Sound:

NW going ebb commences 1500

SE going flood commences 0830, 2100

## DISTANCE

Carrickfergus – Chapel Bay 9.8nm (approx.)

*Entering Chapel Bay beware of Rid Rock. I.C.C. commend that you keep the two red buoys in Donaghadee sound in line and enter when the cottage on the skyline bears 036 <sup>0</sup>(T). 038<sup>0</sup> (M), in transit with a long low cottage on the shore*

Anchor in the west side of the bay, paying careful regard to depths.

*Ensure that you have a good anchor!*

A dinghy will be needed for getting ashore

If the wind is E - SSE we will go to Port Dandy (54<sup>0</sup> 40.54’N, 05<sup>0 </sup>32.5’W)  rather than Chapel Bay. The holding here is not good so anchoring must be done with care.

If the wind is fresh southerly, we will go to Crawfordsburn.

Bring your own food!
